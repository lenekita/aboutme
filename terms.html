General service conditions.

This page presents the main conditions for website creation services by Hélène Nechita. The exact scope, price and timeline are always confirmed in a written quote before the project begins.

Quotes and payment

Quotes are free. A project starts only after the quote has been accepted. The invoice is issued under the French auto-entrepreneur regime.

VAT

Services are invoiced without VAT when the activity is under the French VAT exemption scheme. The invoice may include the legal mention: “TVA non applicable, art. 293 B du CGI”, according to the applicable rules.

Delivery and launch

Depending on the project, delivery may include HTML, CSS and JavaScript files, WordPress setup, GitHub Pages publication, domain connection, contact form setup and essential security recommendations.

Security

Websites are created with careful structure, clean code, HTTPS/domain best practices when available, and simple security habits to reduce unnecessary risk. No website can be guaranteed impossible to attack.
